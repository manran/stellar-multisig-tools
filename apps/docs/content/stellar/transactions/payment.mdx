---
title: Send a payment
description: Prepare a normal Stellar payment and hand the reviewed transaction to the Proposal flow.
---

Payment is the primary transaction flow: choose which Treasury pays, the recipient, the asset, and the amount. Every transaction is reviewed before anyone signs.

1. **Open New proposal → Create payment.** If you started from a Treasury, its address may already be selected.
2. **Enter recipient, asset, and amount.** Use a Stellar G address or saved contact. Credit assets require the destination to accept that asset.
3. **Add context.** A Stellar memo is public. A Private Note is separate workspace information.
4. **Choose the signing window.** This controls how long signers can authorize the prepared transaction.
5. **Select Review payment.** Check the source, recipient, asset, amount, and effects.
6. **Create the Proposal.** Invite signers from the Proposal or let them open it from Inbox. After the required signatures, an authorized participant can submit the transaction.
7. **Check the receipt.** After confirmation, use **View receipt** to see the ledger and transaction hash.

**Review is the boundary.** Before Review, you can change the payment draft. After the Proposal is created, any change to the transaction requires a new transaction and new signatures.

## Reuse a payment with Templates

From the payment form, select **Save as template** to save the source Treasury, recipients, assets, and signing window. Amount and memo are chosen for each request. Open **New proposal** to use a saved Template, or [Templates](/stellar/transactions/templates) to manage access. Template permission management is not part of the New proposal screen.

## Technical details

The composer uses Stellar Payment operations. MultiSig Tools validates the selected asset, destination requirements, source spendability, and fee/reserve constraints before handing the transaction to Review.
